## Presentation Checklist

- [ ] All four members have a meaningful speaking role.
- [ ] Slides tell a clear story from problem through tested solution.
- [ ] Requirements, design, implementation, and test results are connected.
- [ ] Diagrams and text are readable from a distance.
- [ ] The demonstration is rehearsed and has a backup plan.
- [ ] Claims are supported by evidence rather than general statements.
- [ ] Sources for external text, data, and images are cited.
- [ ] The presentation fits the time limit, including transitions.
- [ ] The PowerPoint opens correctly on the presentation computer.
- [ ] No placeholders or template instructions remain.
